Discrete-JEPA sits where three research lineages meet — and where, until now, none of them quite reached on its own. Self-supervised visual representation learning showed you could learn rich features without labels, but settled at the patch level. Discrete image tokenization compressed images into a compact symbolic vocabulary, but anchored that vocabulary to pixel reconstruction. The JEPA family broke the reconstruction habit by predicting in latent space, yet kept its representations continuous, so long autoregressive rollouts quietly drift apart. The cards below trace each thread and the limitation it left behind.

Read together, the threads point at a missing combination. SSL gives a self-supervised signal but no global symbol. Discrete tokenizers give the symbol but anchor it to pixels. The JEPA family gives the semantic latent space but stays continuous and accumulates rollout error. Each lineage's strength is exactly what the next one lacks.

The synthesis
Take a JEPA latent — already trained without a pixel decoder — and discretize its semantic stream with vector quantization. Keep the patch tokens continuous as an intermediate scaffold, and tie the two streams together with a small set of predictive losses so information flows both ways. The result inherits SSL's training signal, the symbol-like compactness of VQ codes, and JEPA's freedom from reconstruction — without inheriting any of the three individual failure modes.
