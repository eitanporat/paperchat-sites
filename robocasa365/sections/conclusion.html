§5 · Takeaways and open problems
p. 10

Three questions ran through the whole study — can one policy learn hundreds of tasks, does pretraining data pay off, and can a robot keep learning without forgetting. Each returns a clean, partly uncomfortable answer.

These findings rest on a deliberately large but deliberately bounded testbed, and two boundaries are worth naming before reading too much into the results.

Where this points
Left standing is a reproducible, openly shared arena — thousands of realistic scenes, hundreds of everyday tasks, thousands of hours of interaction — where the next round of generalist-robot ideas can be trained, stress-tested, and compared on equal footing.
